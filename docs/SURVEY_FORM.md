# MindDiary User Evaluation Survey & Methodology
## Academic Usability & Wellness Impact Assessment

This survey instrument is designed to collect quantitative usability metrics (System Usability Scale - SUS) and qualitative feedback from 15–20 classmates and student evaluators over a 7-day trial period of MindDiary.

---

## 1. Evaluation Methodology

- **Target Audience:** 15–20 undergraduate/graduate university students.
- **Evaluation Period:** 5 to 7 days of daily interaction with MindDiary.
- **Key Tasks Evaluated:**
  1. Creating a daily journal entry with voice dictation and image attachment.
  2. Performing a 10-second wellness check-in and viewing personal insights.
  3. Using the Student Focus Pomodoro timer and exam countdown tracker.
  4. Testing a Calm Room exercise (Box Breathing or Vent Box).
  5. Generating an AI reflection or using the CBT Thought Challenger.
  6. Generating a 7-day Counselor Summary report link.

## 4. Quantitative Analysis Framework (For Final Report Chapter 6)

### 4.1 System Usability Score (SUS) Formula
To compute the standard SUS score from Likert responses:
$$\text{SUS Score} = \left( \sum (\text{Odd Questions} - 1) + \sum (5 - \text{Even Questions}) \right) \times 2.5$$
- **Benchmark Goal:** A score $> 80$ indicates "Grade A" exceptional user usability.
